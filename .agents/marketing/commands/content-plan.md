# Command: content-plan

## Trigger
`/marketing content-plan` oppure "Pianifica i contenuti"

## Processo
1. Carica target personas da `company/customers/segments.md`
2. Identifica temi chiave da roadmap, competitor analysis, feedback clienti
3. Mappa content a fasi del funnel: Awareness → Consideration → Decision
4. Per ogni content: titolo, formato, target persona, keyword, CTA, data pubblicazione
5. Bilancia: thought leadership, product content, case study, SEO plays

## Output
Salva in: `docs/reports/content-plan-{period}.md`
Commit: `[marketing] plan: content calendar for {period}`
