# Command: campaign-brief

## Trigger
`/marketing campaign-brief [campaign]`

## Processo
1. Definisci: obiettivo, target, messaging, canali, budget, timeline, KPI
2. Crea brief strutturato per esecuzione

## Output
Salva in: `docs/reports/campaign-brief-{slug}.md`
Commit: `[marketing] campaign: brief for {campaign}`
